Design and implement a dashboard for a newsletter analytics product.

The user is a solo creator or small media team. They need to understand subscriber growth, retention, revenue, traffic sources, and which recent posts are driving audience value.

Required content:

- A compact overview of the most important metrics.
- Charts or visual summaries for growth, retention, and revenue.
- A recent-posts performance table.
- At least one alert or insight that helps the creator decide what to do next.
- Responsive behavior for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Make it feel like a real operational product, not a decorative analytics mockup.
